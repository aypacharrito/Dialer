# Pacifica CRM sales playbook

## Positioning

Pacifica is the affordable follow-up command center for businesses that depend on leads. It keeps calls, texts, notes, appointments, and pipeline activity together so paid opportunities do not disappear in spreadsheets or personal phones.

Primary promise: **Every lead worked. Every follow-up handled.**

Price punch line: **A complete lead CRM for less than Netflix Premium.**

Lead with outcomes—not “AI.” The product is about faster first contact, consistent follow-up, more appointments, and clear manager visibility. AI is the assistant inside that workflow.

## Best first markets

1. Independent insurance agencies
2. Roofing, solar, HVAC, and remodeling companies
3. Immigration, personal-injury, criminal-defense, and family-law firms
4. Realtors and mortgage brokers
5. Auto dealerships
6. Dental, med-spa, chiropractic, and cosmetic clinics
7. Restoration, pest-control, moving, cleaning, and landscaping companies
8. Tax-resolution, credit-repair, lending, and merchant-service companies

Prioritize companies that are visibly running Google, Meta, Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, or lead-vendor campaigns. They already understand that missed follow-up wastes money.

## Cold call

> Hey, quick question—when a new lead comes in, do you have one system that immediately puts it in front of your team and keeps the follow-up from getting forgotten? I built Pacifica CRM for businesses that depend on leads. It keeps the calls, texts, appointments, and pipeline in one place. I can show you the whole workflow in ten minutes.

If they already have a CRM:

> That makes sense. Is your team actually using it for every call and follow-up, or are leads still ending up in spreadsheets and personal phones? Pacifica is built to make the daily work simple, and it starts at $25.

## Text or direct message

> Hey, I built Pacifica CRM for businesses that depend on leads. It keeps every new inquiry, call, text, appointment, and follow-up in one place so opportunities don’t get forgotten. It starts at $25/month. Open to a quick demo?

## Ten-minute demo

1. Import a real or sample lead list.
2. Show the Priority and General queues.
3. Open a contact and show source, lead cost, notes, consent, and follow-up.
4. Place one browser call and save the outcome.
5. Open Messages and generate a human-reviewed follow-up.
6. Show the Pipeline and Reports screens.
7. End with the customer’s current lead volume and the value of recovering one missed opportunity.

## Discovery questions

- How many new leads do you receive each month?
- Where do they come from, and what does each lead cost?
- How quickly does the first call usually happen?
- How many attempts does the team make before giving up?
- Where are texts, notes, and appointment dates stored today?
- Can a manager see untouched leads without asking the team?
- What is one completed sale or retained customer worth?

## Offer

Use a guided setup instead of an open-ended free trial:

> I’ll configure Pacifica around your lead sources, import your first list, and show your team the workflow. If it doesn’t make follow-up clearer, don’t keep it.

Do not promise a specific number of sales. Demonstrate faster response, consistent follow-up, and measurable activity.

## Daily prospecting target

- Build a focused list of 25 local businesses in one industry.
- Call 15 owners or managers.
- Send 10 personalized follow-ups only where contact is lawful.
- Book 2 demos.
- Record the objection and outcome after every attempt.
- Improve one part of the pitch each day based on real conversations.

## Compliance

Use Pacifica only with lawful lead sources. Preserve consent records, honor STOP and company-specific Do Not Call requests, follow applicable calling-hour and recording rules, and do not use caller-ID rotation or automation to evade carrier or consumer-protection controls.
